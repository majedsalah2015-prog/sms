# 04 — Glossary

**Phase:** 1 — Foundation | **Status:** Draft for review | **Owner:** Senior Business Analyst

> Canonical bilingual vocabulary. Every module document, screen label, and report must use these terms exactly. Additions require updating this glossary first (single source of truth).

---

## 1. Academic structure

| Term (EN) | المصطلح (AR) | Definition |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| School | مدرسة | A licensed educational institution; the tenant scope of all data |
| Academic Year | عام دراسي | The annual cycle (e.g., 2026–2027) scoping all transactions |
| Semester | فصل دراسي | Major division of an academic year (typically 2–3) |
| Term | فترة تقييم | Assessment period within a semester (configurable) |
| Stage | مرحلة دراسية | Educational stage: KG, Elementary, Intermediate, Secondary |
| Grade (level) | صف | Year level within a stage (e.g., Grade 5) |
| Section | شعبة | A class group within a grade (e.g., 5-A) |
| Classroom | قاعة / فصل | A physical room with capacity and resources |
| Subject | مادة دراسية | A course of study (e.g., Mathematics) |
| Curriculum | منهج | The program of study (national, IGCSE, American…) |
| Timetable | الجدول الدراسي | Weekly schedule of periods per section |
| Period | حصة | A single teaching slot in the timetable |
| Teacher Assignment | إسناد المواد | Linking a teacher to subject + section for a year |
| Academic Calendar | التقويم الدراسي | Working days, holidays, and events of a year |
| Rollover / Year-End Closing | إقفال العام الدراسي | Guided transition from one academic year to the next |
| Promotion | ترفيع | Moving a student to the next grade after passing |
| Retention | إعادة صف | Keeping a student in the same grade |

## 8. Conventions

- English is the documentation language; Arabic terms above are the **binding UI translations**.
- "Grade" always means grade level (صف); the score meaning is always "mark/grade result" (تقدير) to avoid ambiguity.
- "Parent" is the entity; "Guardian" is used only for legal custody distinct from parents.
